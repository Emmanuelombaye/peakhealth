Frequently Asked Questions

How are PeakBody research peptides verified?

Every synthesis batch undergoes quantitative and qualitative Reverse-Phase HPLC and ESI-MS mass spectrometry analysis. Each order includes an independent 3rd-Party Certificate of Analysis (COA) confirming >99% purity.

What packaging and storage formats are used?

All peptides are supplied in lyophilized (freeze-dried) powder form inside sterile 3ml glass vials sealed with rubber stoppers and aluminum crimp caps, packaged in light-shielding boxes.

Orders are dispatched with same-day shipping from our Tempe, Arizona facility. Call 1-800-249-4566 for scientific support.

Are research peptides intended for human consumption?

No. All PeakBody Peptides products are strictly sold for laboratory in-vitro research, analytical, and educational purposes only.
